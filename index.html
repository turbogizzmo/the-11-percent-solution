      How it works
      • What should I buy? builds a basket from everyday things people use: filters, salt, bulbs, batteries, cleaning supplies, tools. Tap Shuffle for a different mix, 🔒 to keep an item, ↻ to swap one.
      • Prices marked "about" are typical prices. The rest are real Menards.com prices from the date shown, and they can still differ by store.
      • Start shopping keeps a running total in the store, so you know exactly what's left on the check, and suggests small things that fit what's left.
      • With your own list, you're the menu. Add things you really would buy, with today's prices. Set how many of each you'd take with the stepper.
      • Tap Must buy on anything that has to be in the cart. Uncheck an item to leave it out without deleting it.
      • Matches add sales tax so the total lines up with the register, then show what's left on the check or what you'd pay.
      • Your list stays on this device. Share my list makes a link someone else can open to copy it.
      • Prices change and vary by store, so double-check at the shelf before you check out.
